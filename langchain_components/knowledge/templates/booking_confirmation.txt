===== booking_confirmation.txt =====

BOOKING CONFIRMATION TEMPLATE

This document provides the standard template used to confirm a customer's booking on Elixway. Placeholders shown in double curly braces are populated dynamically with actual booking data by the backend system at the time of message generation.

TEMPLATE

Subject: Your Elixway booking is confirmed

Hello {{customer_name}},

Your booking with Elixway has been confirmed. Please find your trip details below.

Booking ID: {{booking_id}}
Pickup Location: {{pickup}}
Drop Location: {{drop}}
Pickup Time: {{pickup_time}}
Vehicle: {{vehicle}}
Estimated Fare: {{estimated_fare}}

Driver Details:
Driver Name: {{driver_name}}

Driver contact information and live tracking details will be shared closer to your scheduled pickup time.

If you need to make changes to your booking or have any questions, please contact our support team at {{support_number}} or through the Elixway app.

Thank you for choosing Elixway.

USAGE NOTES

This template is intended for use immediately after a booking is successfully confirmed through the app, website, or AI Booking Assistant. It should be sent through the customer's preferred notification channel, which may include in-app notification, SMS, or email, depending on account settings.

FIELD DEFINITIONS

- {{customer_name}}: The name of the customer associated with the booking.
- {{booking_id}}: The unique identifier assigned to the booking, used for all future reference and support inquiries.
- {{pickup}}: The specified pickup location for the trip.
- {{drop}}: The specified drop location for the trip, where applicable.
- {{pickup_time}}: The scheduled date and time for pickup.
- {{vehicle}}: The vehicle category selected or assigned for the booking, such as sedan, SUV, Innova, tempo traveller, or luxury.
- {{estimated_fare}}: The estimated fare generated by the backend pricing system at the time of booking confirmation.
- {{driver_name}}: The name of the assigned driver, where already allocated at the time of confirmation. If not yet assigned, this field may be left blank or updated in a follow-up message.
- {{support_number}}: The customer support contact number relevant to the customer's booking or region.

IMPORTANT NOTES FOR AI ASSISTANT USAGE

When referencing this template in conversation, the AI assistant should retrieve actual values for each placeholder from the backend booking system rather than generating placeholder text visible to the customer. This template is a structural reference for how confirmation messages are formatted and should not be presented to customers with unpopulated placeholders.
