RENTAL CAB FAQ

This document answers customer questions related to Elixway rental cab bookings, which allow customers to hire a vehicle with a driver for a specific duration or distance, typically within a single city.

Question: What is a rental cab booking?
Answer: A rental cab booking provides a vehicle with a driver for a defined package, usually measured in hours and kilometers, such as an 8-hour or 4-hour package. This is suitable for local sightseeing, business meetings, or multiple stops within a city without booking separate point-to-point trips.

Question: What happens if I exceed the included hours or kilometers?
Answer: If usage exceeds the hours or kilometers included in the selected rental package, additional charges apply based on the extra hours and kilometers used. These charges are calculated automatically by the backend fare system at the end of the trip and are not fixed in advance.

Question: Can I extend my rental package during the trip?
Answer: Yes, extensions can usually be requested during the trip through the app or by contacting the driver and customer support, subject to driver and vehicle availability for the extended duration.

Question: Does the rental package include waiting time?
Answer: Standard rental packages generally include a reasonable amount of waiting or idle time as part of the package duration, since the vehicle remains with the customer for the full booked period. Extended waiting beyond the package duration may be billed as additional time.

Question: Can I use a rental cab for an outstation trip?
Answer: Rental cabs are primarily designed for within-city usage. For trips that involve traveling to another city, an Outstation Cab booking is more appropriate, as it accounts for one-way or round-trip long-distance travel, driver allowance, and applicable outstation charges.

Question: What vehicle types are available for rental bookings?
Answer: Rental bookings typically offer sedan, SUV, and tempo traveller options, depending on group size and luggage needs. Vehicle availability for rental bookings depends on the city and time slot selected.

Question: Is fuel included in the rental package price?
Answer: Yes, standard rental package pricing includes fuel costs for the distance covered within the package. Additional kilometers driven beyond the package limit are charged separately, which may include a per-kilometer rate covering fuel and other operational costs.

Question: Can I make multiple stops during a rental booking?
Answer: Yes. Rental bookings are designed to accommodate multiple stops within the booked duration, making them suitable for city tours, shopping trips, or meetings at several locations.

Question: What if I finish my trip early?
Answer: If the rental trip concludes before the full package duration or kilometer limit is used, the booking is typically closed at actual usage, though refund eligibility for unused package value depends on the specific package terms shown at the time of booking.

Question: Are night charges applicable to rental bookings?
Answer: Night charges may apply if the rental booking starts or extends into designated night hours, as outlined in the night charges policy document. This is calculated automatically based on the trip timing.

Question: Can I book a rental cab for a full day?
Answer: Yes, full-day rental packages are available, typically covering a set number of hours such as 8 or 10 hours along with an included kilometer allowance, suitable for extended local travel needs.
