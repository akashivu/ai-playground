WAITING CHARGES POLICY

This document explains how waiting time is handled and charged across Elixway trip types, including Airport Transfers, Outstation Cabs, and Rental Cabs.

STANDARD FREE WAITING TIME

Each booking type includes a standard allowance of free waiting time at the pickup location, intended to give customers reasonable time to reach the vehicle after the driver's arrival. This allowance is generally longer for airport transfers, accounting for time needed to exit the terminal and locate the vehicle, compared to standard local pickups.

WHEN WAITING CHARGES APPLY

Once the free waiting time allowance is exceeded, waiting charges may begin to accrue for each additional period the driver waits beyond the included allowance. This applies in situations such as:

- A customer not being ready at the pickup location when the driver arrives.
- Delays in exiting the airport terminal beyond the included allowance for airport transfers.
- Extended stops during rental or outstation trips beyond what is reasonably expected for the booked package.

HOW WAITING CHARGES ARE CALCULATED

Waiting charges are typically calculated on a per-minute or per-block basis once the free allowance is exceeded, and are calculated automatically by the backend fare system based on actual wait duration recorded through the trip timeline. The applicable rate depends on the vehicle category and city.

RENTAL PACKAGE WAITING TIME

For rental cab bookings, waiting time is generally included as part of the overall package duration, since the vehicle remains engaged with the customer throughout the booked hours. Waiting charges in this context typically apply only if the customer requests the vehicle to wait beyond the total package duration.

NO-SHOW AND EXTENDED WAITING

If a customer does not appear within an extended period after the free waiting allowance and does not respond to contact attempts from the driver or support, the booking may be treated under the no-show provisions outlined in the cancellation policy, which may involve charges beyond standard waiting fees.

EXCEPTIONS

Waiting charges may be waived or adjusted in specific circumstances, such as documented flight delays affecting airport pickups, verified medical emergencies, or situations where the delay was caused by a platform or driver-related issue rather than the customer. Waivers are reviewed and approved by customer support on a case-by-case basis.

TRANSPARENCY IN PRICING

Applicable waiting charges are reflected as a separate line item in the final trip invoice, allowing customers to see the exact duration and rate applied beyond the included free waiting allowance.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For disputes regarding waiting charges, or to request a waiver based on documented circumstances, customers should contact Elixway customer support with their booking ID and relevant details.
