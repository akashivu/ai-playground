===== suv.txt =====

VEHICLE PROFILE: SUV

This document describes the SUV vehicle category available on Elixway, including capacity, suitability, and typical use cases. Exact vehicle models and live availability are determined by the backend fleet system at the time of booking.

PASSENGER CAPACITY

SUVs on Elixway typically accommodate up to six passengers comfortably, in addition to the driver, depending on the specific model. This makes the category suitable for medium-sized families or small groups traveling together.

LUGGAGE CAPACITY

SUVs generally offer greater boot space compared to sedans, typically accommodating two to three medium or large suitcases along with additional smaller bags. This makes SUVs a practical choice for trips involving more substantial luggage than a standard sedan can comfortably hold.

COMFORT LEVEL

SUVs provide elevated seating position, additional legroom, and generally a smoother ride on uneven or hilly terrain compared to sedans, making them a popular choice for both city travel and outstation trips to hill destinations.

RECOMMENDED USAGE

The SUV category is commonly recommended for:

- Medium-sized family trips requiring more passenger and luggage space than a sedan.
- Outstation trips to hill stations or destinations with varied road conditions.
- Airport transfers for groups with moderate to heavy luggage.
- Rental bookings involving multiple stops with a group of four to six passengers.

SUITABLE TRIP TYPES

SUVs are well suited for outstation trips to destinations with hilly or uneven terrain, family vacations, and airport transfers for groups needing extra luggage space. They offer a practical middle ground between sedans and larger vehicles like tempo travellers.

IDEAL TRAVEL SCENARIOS

- A family of five or six traveling together for a weekend getaway.
- A group heading to a hill station destination where road conditions benefit from higher ground clearance.
- Travelers with substantial luggage, such as multiple suitcases or equipment, needing more boot space than a sedan offers.
- Corporate groups requiring a comfortable vehicle for short business trips with several colleagues.

LIMITATIONS

While SUVs offer more space than sedans, they may not be the most practical choice for very large groups exceeding six passengers or for trips requiring maximum luggage capacity, where a tempo traveller would be more appropriate.

AVAILABILITY

SUV availability, exact fare, and estimated arrival time are determined dynamically by the backend booking system based on real-time demand, vehicle type, and location, and are displayed to the customer during the booking process.
