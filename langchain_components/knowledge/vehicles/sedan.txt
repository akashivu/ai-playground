VEHICLE PROFILE: SEDAN

This document describes the sedan vehicle category available on Elixway, including capacity, suitability, and typical use cases. Exact vehicle models and live availability are determined by the backend fleet system at the time of booking.

PASSENGER CAPACITY

Sedans on Elixway typically accommodate up to four passengers comfortably, in addition to the driver. This makes the category well suited for individuals, couples, or small families traveling together.

LUGGAGE CAPACITY

Standard sedans generally offer boot space suitable for one to two medium-sized suitcases along with small carry-on bags or backpacks. Customers with more than two large suitcases or bulky items are encouraged to consider an SUV or Innova-class vehicle to ensure comfortable luggage accommodation.

COMFORT LEVEL

Sedans provide a standard level of comfort suitable for short to medium-distance travel, with air conditioning and adequate legroom for four passengers. For long-distance outstation travel, sedans remain a popular choice due to fuel efficiency and manageable size on highways.

RECOMMENDED USAGE

The sedan category is commonly recommended for:

- Airport transfers for individuals or small groups with standard luggage.
- Local city travel and short rental bookings.
- Outstation trips for smaller groups seeking a balance of comfort and affordability.
- Business travel where a professional, comfortable vehicle is preferred without the need for extra space.

SUITABLE TRIP TYPES

Sedans are well suited for point-to-point airport transfers, short rental packages within a city, and outstation trips covering moderate distances. They are generally not the ideal choice for large groups, extensive luggage, or trips requiring maximum passenger space such as family outings with more than four travelers.

IDEAL TRAVEL SCENARIOS

- A business traveler needing a reliable airport pickup with one or two bags.
- A couple planning a weekend outstation trip to a nearby city.
- A small family of up to four members traveling locally for errands or short excursions.
- Customers prioritizing fuel efficiency and a smoother ride on highway stretches during outstation travel.

LIMITATIONS

Sedans are not recommended for groups exceeding four passengers or trips involving substantial luggage, sports equipment, or bulky items. For such requirements, customers should consider the SUV, Innova, or tempo traveller categories, depending on group size.

AVAILABILITY

Sedan availability, exact fare, and estimated arrival time are determined dynamically by the backend booking system based on real-time demand and location, and are displayed to the customer during the booking process.
