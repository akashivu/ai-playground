

AIRPORT TRANSFER PRICING STRUCTURE

This document explains how airport transfer fares are structured on Elixway. It is intended to help the AI assistant explain pricing concepts to customers. It does not contain live fare figures, as actual pricing is generated dynamically by the backend fare calculation system based on real-time factors.

FARE COMPONENTS

An airport transfer fare is generally composed of the following components:

- Base fare, determined by the distance between the pickup location and the airport, or between the airport and the drop location, along with the selected vehicle category.
- Applicable toll charges along the route, where relevant.
- Airport entry or pickup fees charged by the airport authority for vehicles accessing designated pickup zones, where applicable.
- Night charges, if the pickup falls within the designated night hours window, as described in the night charges policy.
- Waiting charges, if applicable, once the included free waiting time is exceeded.

FACTORS INFLUENCING FARE

Several factors influence the final fare shown to a customer at the time of booking, including:

- Distance between pickup and drop locations.
- Vehicle category selected, such as sedan, SUV, Innova, or luxury.
- Time of day, particularly whether the trip falls within night charge hours.
- Real-time demand conditions in the city at the time of booking.
- Specific airport-related charges applicable to the departure or arrival airport.

ONE-WAY VERSUS ROUND TRIP

Airport transfers are typically booked as one-way trips, either from a location to the airport or from the airport to a location. Customers requiring both a drop and a pickup, such as at the start and end of a trip, generally need to book two separate airport transfer trips, unless a combined package option is offered for a specific route.

ESTIMATED FARE DISPLAY

Before confirming a booking, customers are shown an estimated fare based on the details entered, including pickup and drop location, vehicle category, and scheduled time. This estimate is generated by the backend pricing engine using live data and is not manually set within static documentation.

FINAL FARE VARIATIONS

The final fare charged at the end of a trip may vary slightly from the initial estimate due to factors such as route changes requested by the customer, additional waiting time, or applicable night charges if the actual trip timing shifts due to flight delays. Any such variation is reflected transparently in the final trip invoice.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For questions about how a specific airport transfer fare was calculated, or to raise a concern about a charged amount, customers should contact Elixway customer support with their booking ID for a detailed fare breakdown review.
