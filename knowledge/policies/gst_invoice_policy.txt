

GST INVOICE POLICY

This document explains how Goods and Services Tax (GST) is applied to Elixway bookings and the process for obtaining a GST-compliant invoice.

GST APPLICABILITY

GST is applicable on services provided through the Elixway platform in accordance with applicable tax regulations. The applicable GST rate and its breakdown are calculated automatically by the backend billing system based on the service type and are reflected in the fare breakdown before booking confirmation and in the final invoice.

STANDARD INVOICE

Every completed booking generates a standard digital invoice accessible through the Booking History section of the app, which includes the base fare, applicable additional charges such as tolls or driver allowance, and the GST amount charged, where applicable.

REQUESTING A GST INVOICE WITH BUSINESS DETAILS

Customers who require a formal GST invoice with their business name and GST Identification Number (GSTIN) for corporate reimbursement or accounting purposes can typically request this by entering their GST details either at the time of booking, if the option is available, or by submitting a request through customer support after trip completion.

INFORMATION REQUIRED FOR GST INVOICES

To generate a GST invoice with business details, customers are generally required to provide accurate information, including:

- Registered business name
- GST Identification Number (GSTIN)
- Business address as registered with tax authorities

Providing inaccurate or incomplete GST details may result in delays or the invoice being issued only with standard consumer details.

TIMELINE FOR GST DETAIL UPDATES

Requests to add or correct GST details on an invoice should ideally be made as soon as possible after trip completion, since invoices may not be modifiable after a certain period due to accounting and compliance requirements. Customers should check the specific timeline permitted for such changes through customer support.

CORPORATE AND BUSINESS ACCOUNTS

Customers with recurring business travel needs may be eligible for a corporate account setup, which can streamline GST invoicing across multiple bookings. Interested customers should contact Elixway customer support to inquire about corporate account options.

INVOICE CORRECTIONS

If a customer identifies an error in an issued invoice, such as incorrect GST details or fare discrepancies, they should raise the issue with customer support promptly, providing the booking ID and a description of the required correction.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For any questions regarding GST applicability, invoice generation, or corrections to existing invoices, customers should contact Elixway customer support with their booking ID and relevant details.
