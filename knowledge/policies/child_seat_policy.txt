

CHILD SEAT POLICY

This document outlines Elixway's approach to child seat availability and safety guidelines for customers traveling with young children.

AVAILABILITY OF CHILD SEATS

Child seats may be available as an optional add-on for select bookings, subject to city-level availability and advance request. Customers requiring a child seat should indicate this requirement at the time of booking, where the option is available, or contact customer support in advance to confirm availability for their specific trip.

ADVANCE REQUEST RECOMMENDED

Since child seat availability depends on inventory and driver preparation, it is strongly recommended that customers request a child seat at least a few hours in advance, and ideally at the time of initial booking, particularly for airport transfers and outstation trips where planning ahead reduces the risk of unavailability.

AGE AND SIZE CONSIDERATIONS

Child seat requirements generally vary based on the child's age, weight, and height, consistent with standard road safety guidance. Customers should specify the approximate age or size of the child when requesting a child seat so that an appropriately sized seat can be arranged where available.

IF A CHILD SEAT IS NOT AVAILABLE

In situations where a requested child seat is not available for a specific trip due to inventory constraints, customer support will inform the customer as early as possible so alternative arrangements can be made, such as the customer bringing their own child seat or rescheduling the trip if feasible.

CUSTOMER-PROVIDED CHILD SEATS

Customers are welcome to bring and use their own child seat during a trip. Drivers are expected to provide reasonable assistance with the installation of a customer-provided child seat, though the customer remains responsible for ensuring correct and safe installation.

SAFETY RESPONSIBILITY

While Elixway aims to support safe travel for children through available child seat options, the ultimate responsibility for ensuring appropriate child restraint use, in line with applicable local road safety regulations, rests with the accompanying adult passenger.

ADDITIONAL CHARGES

Where child seats are provided as an add-on service, a nominal additional charge may apply, which is displayed to the customer at the time of request or booking. This charge is separate from the base trip fare.

MULTIPLE CHILD SEATS

For families requiring more than one child seat, availability may be more limited and should be confirmed well in advance through customer support, particularly for larger vehicle categories such as SUVs or tempo travellers accommodating bigger groups.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For questions regarding child seat availability, size compatibility, or to arrange a child seat for an upcoming trip, customers should contact Elixway customer support with their booking details as early as possible.
