===== refund_policy.txt =====

REFUND POLICY

This document outlines how refunds are processed for bookings made through Elixway, including timelines, eligibility, and the process for requesting a refund.

REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Refunds may be issued under the following circumstances:

- Full or partial cancellation of a booking in accordance with the cancellation policy, where the amount already paid exceeds any applicable cancellation charge.
- Payment failures where an amount was deducted from the customer's account but the booking was not successfully confirmed.
- Service issues verified by customer support, such as no driver being assigned within a reasonable time, significant deviation from the agreed itinerary due to platform error, or a vehicle not matching the confirmed category without customer consent.
- Duplicate charges resulting from technical errors during the payment process.

REFUND PROCESS

Once a refund is approved, it is processed to the original payment method used for the transaction. Refunds are not issued in cash or to a different account or payment method unless the original method is no longer valid, in which case customer support will coordinate an alternative resolution.

REFUND TIMELINE

Refund processing timelines depend on the payment method and the customer's bank or payment provider. As a general guide:

- Digital wallet and UPI refunds are typically processed within a few business days.
- Card refunds may take longer to reflect, depending on the issuing bank's processing cycle.
- Net banking refunds follow similar timelines to card refunds.

Exact refund status and expected timelines for a specific transaction are available through the Payment History section of the app or by contacting customer support.

NON-REFUNDABLE COMPONENTS

Certain charges may be non-refundable even when a refund is approved for the base fare, including:

- Toll charges or permit fees already incurred if the trip had partially begun.
- Cancellation charges applicable under the cancellation policy.
- Charges related to services already rendered, such as completed portions of a multi-day outstation trip.

HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

Customers can request a refund review by navigating to the relevant booking in Booking History and selecting the support or help option, or by directly contacting Elixway customer support with the booking ID and a description of the issue. Support agents will review the transaction and fare breakdown before approving or denying the refund request.

DISPUTED REFUNDS

If a customer disagrees with a refund decision, they may request an escalation through customer support. Escalated cases are reviewed by a senior support agent, and the customer will be informed of the outcome along with the reasoning behind the decision.

REFUND STATUS TRACKING

Once a refund is initiated, its status can be tracked through the Payment History section of the app, which reflects updates provided by the payment processor. If a refund does not reflect within the expected timeline stated by support, customers should follow up with the transaction reference number for further investigation.
