

PET POLICY

This document outlines the guidelines for traveling with pets on trips booked through Elixway, applicable across Airport Transfers, Outstation Cabs, and Rental Cabs.

GENERAL GUIDELINES

Elixway allows pets to travel with customers on eligible bookings, subject to certain conditions intended to ensure the safety and comfort of the pet, the customer, other passengers, and the driver. Customers are encouraged to indicate the presence of a pet at the time of booking so that an appropriate vehicle and driver can be arranged.

DECLARING A PET AT BOOKING

Customers traveling with a pet should mention this during the booking process, if the option is available, or communicate it directly with the driver or customer support after booking. This allows Elixway to consider driver preference, vehicle suitability, and any additional requirements needed for a comfortable trip.

PET CARRIER REQUIREMENTS

For safety reasons, small pets are generally expected to travel in an appropriate carrier or pet crate, particularly during outstation and airport transfer trips. Larger pets may be permitted to travel without a carrier at driver discretion, provided they remain calm and under the customer's control throughout the trip, often with appropriate restraint such as a harness.

DRIVER DISCRETION

While Elixway generally supports pet travel, individual drivers may have personal comfort levels or allergies related to animals. In cases where an assigned driver is not comfortable transporting a pet, customer support will attempt to reassign a suitable driver where possible, subject to availability.

CLEANLINESS AND VEHICLE CONDITION

Customers are responsible for ensuring their pet does not cause damage or significant soiling to the vehicle interior. In the event of damage or excessive cleaning being required due to a pet, additional charges may apply, consistent with general vehicle damage and cleaning provisions.

SERVICE ANIMALS

Trained service animals accompanying customers with disabilities are accommodated in accordance with applicable local regulations, and standard pet carrier requirements may not apply in such cases. Customers are encouraged to inform Elixway in advance if a service animal will be traveling.

RESTRICTIONS

Certain vehicle categories or specific trip types may have limitations on pet travel due to space constraints or other operational factors. Availability of pet-friendly vehicles may vary by city and should be confirmed at the time of booking where possible.

AIRPORT TRANSFER CONSIDERATIONS

For airport transfers involving pet travel, customers should also be aware of and comply with airline-specific pet travel policies and any documentation required by the airport or airline, as these are governed separately from Elixway's transportation service.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For questions about pet travel eligibility for a specific booking, or to report an issue related to pet travel, customers should contact Elixway customer support with their booking ID.
