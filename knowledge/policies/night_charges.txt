

NIGHT CHARGES POLICY

This document explains when and why night charges are applied to trips booked through Elixway, covering Airport Transfers, Outstation Cabs, and Rental Cabs.

WHAT ARE NIGHT CHARGES

Night charges are an additional fee applied to trips that begin, end, or substantially occur during designated night hours. This charge accounts for the increased operational cost of arranging drivers and vehicles during late-night and early-morning periods.

WHEN NIGHT CHARGES APPLY

Night charges are generally applicable when a trip's pickup time falls within a defined night window, commonly covering late night through early morning hours. The exact night window may vary slightly by city and is defined within the backend pricing configuration rather than fixed universally across all locations.

Common scenarios where night charges apply include:

- Airport transfers scheduled for early morning departures or late-night arrivals.
- Outstation trips beginning or ending during night hours.
- Rental bookings that start within the night charge window, even if the majority of the trip occurs during daytime hours.

HOW NIGHT CHARGES ARE CALCULATED

Night charges are typically calculated as either a fixed additional fee or a percentage addition to the base fare, depending on the service type and city. This charge is calculated automatically by the backend fare system based on the scheduled or actual pickup time and is displayed to the customer before booking confirmation.

MULTI-DAY TRIPS AND NIGHT CHARGES

For multi-day outstation or rental trips, night charges may apply specifically to segments of the journey that occur during night hours, such as an early morning start on the first day, rather than being applied uniformly across the entire trip duration.

EXCEPTIONS

Night charges are generally not negotiable or waivable, as they reflect a standard operational cost applied consistently across eligible bookings. However, in cases of billing errors, such as a night charge being applied to a trip that did not actually fall within the night window due to a scheduling or system error, customers may request a review through customer support.

TRANSPARENCY IN PRICING

Night charges, where applicable, are shown as a separate line item in the fare estimate before booking confirmation and in the final invoice, allowing customers to understand this charge distinctly from the base fare and other applicable fees.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For questions about whether a specific pickup time falls within the night charge window, or to dispute an applied night charge, customers should contact Elixway customer support with their booking ID for review.
