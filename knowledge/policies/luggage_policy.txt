

LUGGAGE POLICY

This document outlines standard luggage handling guidelines for trips booked through Elixway, applicable across Airport Transfers, Outstation Cabs, and Rental Cabs.

GENERAL GUIDELINES

Each vehicle category has a defined luggage capacity based on its boot space and passenger configuration. Customers are encouraged to select a vehicle category that comfortably accommodates their expected luggage volume, particularly for airport transfers and outstation trips involving suitcases or bulky items. Refer to individual vehicle documents for specific luggage capacity guidance for sedans, SUVs, Innova-class vehicles, tempo travellers, and luxury vehicles.

STANDARD LUGGAGE ALLOWANCE

Standard bookings typically accommodate luggage consistent with the passenger capacity of the selected vehicle, such as one medium to large suitcase and one carry-on bag per passenger for sedan and SUV categories. Larger vehicles such as tempo travellers offer significantly higher luggage capacity suited for group travel.

EXCESS OR OVERSIZED LUGGAGE

If a customer's luggage volume exceeds what the selected vehicle can reasonably accommodate, this may result in one of the following outcomes:

- The driver may be unable to fit all items, requiring the customer to leave certain items behind or arrange alternative transport for excess luggage.
- Customers with known excess luggage requirements are strongly encouraged to select a larger vehicle category at the time of booking to avoid last-minute issues.
- In some cases, an on-the-spot vehicle upgrade may be arranged through customer support, subject to availability and applicable fare adjustment.

RESTRICTED AND PROHIBITED ITEMS

Certain items may not be transported due to safety regulations or driver discretion, including but not limited to hazardous materials, flammable substances, illegal goods, and items prohibited under applicable transport regulations. Customers are responsible for ensuring their luggage complies with all applicable laws, particularly for airport transfers where airline and airport security regulations also apply.

FRAGILE AND VALUABLE ITEMS

Elixway drivers exercise reasonable care in handling luggage; however, customers are responsible for the safety of fragile or high-value items during transport. It is recommended that customers personally handle exceptionally fragile or valuable belongings rather than placing them in the vehicle boot.

LOST OR DAMAGED LUGGAGE

If a customer believes luggage was lost or damaged during a trip, they should report the issue to Elixway customer support as soon as possible, ideally before leaving the pickup or drop location, along with relevant details such as booking ID, description of the item, and circumstances. Reported cases are reviewed individually, and resolution depends on the specific circumstances of the incident.

LUGGAGE ASSISTANCE

Drivers are generally expected to provide reasonable assistance with loading and unloading standard luggage. Assistance with unusually heavy or specialized equipment, such as sports gear or musical instruments, should be communicated in advance so that an appropriate vehicle and driver expectation can be set.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For questions regarding luggage capacity for a specific vehicle type, or to report a luggage-related issue, customers should contact Elixway customer support with their booking ID.
