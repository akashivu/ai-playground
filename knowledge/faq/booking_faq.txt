BOOKING FAQ

This document answers common customer questions related to creating, modifying, and understanding bookings on Elixway. It is intended to support the AI Booking Assistant and customer support agents in providing accurate, consistent answers.

Question: How do I book a ride on Elixway?
Answer: You can book a ride by opening the Elixway app or website, entering your pickup and drop locations, selecting a service type such as Airport Transfer, Outstation Cab, or Rental Cab, choosing a vehicle category, and confirming the booking. The AI Trip Planner can also assist by generating suggested vehicle options and travel plans, but final vehicle availability and pricing are always confirmed through the backend booking system.

Question: Can I book a ride for someone else?
Answer: Yes. During the booking process, you can enter a different traveler name and contact number in the passenger details section. The primary account holder remains responsible for payment and booking management unless ownership is transferred through customer support.

Question: How far in advance can I book a ride?
Answer: Airport transfers and outstation cabs can generally be booked in advance, ranging from a few hours to several days, depending on vehicle availability in the selected city. Rental cabs may have different advance booking windows depending on demand. Exact availability windows are determined by the backend scheduling system at the time of booking.

Question: Can I book a round trip?
Answer: Yes. Outstation and rental bookings support round trip options, where the same vehicle and driver may be assigned for both onward and return journeys, subject to availability. Airport transfers are typically booked as one-way trips, though a return airport transfer can be scheduled separately.

Question: What happens after I confirm a booking?
Answer: Once a booking is confirmed, you will receive a confirmation message containing your booking ID, vehicle details, driver information, and pickup time. Driver assignment and live tracking details are typically shared closer to the pickup time through the app or SMS.

Question: Can I modify my booking after confirming it?
Answer: Modifications such as changing pickup time, pickup location, or vehicle type may be possible before the trip begins, subject to availability and applicable policies. Significant changes, such as altering the destination city for an outstation trip, may require cancelling the existing booking and creating a new one. Refer to the cancellation policy for details on charges that may apply.

Question: What if my flight is delayed and I have an airport transfer booked?
Answer: Elixway airport transfer bookings account for reasonable flight delays through real-time flight tracking integration where available. If a significant delay occurs, contact customer support as early as possible so the driver assignment and pickup time can be adjusted.

Question: How do I know if my driver has been assigned?
Answer: Driver assignment details, including driver name, contact number, and vehicle number, are shared through the app and via SMS once a driver has been allocated. This usually happens closer to the scheduled pickup time.

Question: Can I choose a specific driver or vehicle?
Answer: Specific driver or exact vehicle unit requests cannot be guaranteed, as assignment is handled by the backend dispatch system based on availability, location, and vehicle category. Customers can select the vehicle category and type, such as sedan, SUV, or tempo traveller.

Question: What should I do if I face an issue during my ride?
Answer: For any issue during an ongoing ride, use the in-app support option or contact the Elixway support number provided in your booking confirmation. Customer support can assist with driver communication, route concerns, or emergency situations.

Question: Is booking confirmation guaranteed immediately?
Answer: Most bookings are confirmed instantly once payment or booking details are submitted. In cases of high demand or limited vehicle availability, confirmation may take a short additional time while the system allocates a vehicle.
