

ACCOUNT FAQ

This document addresses customer questions related to creating, managing, and securing an Elixway user account.

Question: How do I create an Elixway account?
Answer: An account can be created through the Elixway app or website by providing a valid phone number or email address and completing a verification step, typically through a one-time password. Basic profile details such as name can be added after initial signup.

Question: Do I need an account to book a ride?
Answer: In most cases, creating an account is required to book a ride, as it allows Elixway to manage booking history, payment methods, and customer support requests associated with your profile. Guest booking options, if available, may have limited functionality compared to a registered account.

Question: How do I update my profile information?
Answer: Profile information such as name, email address, or emergency contact details can be updated through the Account or Profile section of the app. Changes to the registered phone number may require additional verification for security purposes.

Question: I am not receiving the OTP for login. What should I do?
Answer: If an OTP is not received, check that your phone has network connectivity and that the correct number is entered. Wait a short period before requesting a new OTP, as multiple rapid requests may be temporarily restricted. If the issue continues, contact customer support for manual verification assistance.

Question: Can I have multiple accounts with the same phone number?
Answer: Each phone number is generally linked to a single Elixway account to maintain consistency in booking history and support records. If you believe duplicate accounts exist, contact customer support to review and consolidate account details.

Question: How do I delete my Elixway account?
Answer: Account deletion requests can typically be submitted through the app settings or by contacting customer support directly. Deletion requests are processed according to applicable data protection regulations, and certain booking or transaction records may be retained as required by law even after account deletion.

Question: Is my personal information secure with Elixway?
Answer: Elixway follows standard data protection practices to safeguard customer information, including encrypted storage of sensitive details and restricted internal access. Personal information is used to facilitate bookings, communication, and support, and is not shared with unauthorized third parties.

Question: Can I link multiple payment methods to my account?
Answer: Yes, multiple payment methods such as cards, UPI IDs, or digital wallets can typically be saved to an account for convenience during checkout, subject to platform support for each method.

Question: How do I change my registered mobile number?
Answer: Changing a registered mobile number usually requires verifying the new number through an OTP process within the Account Settings section. If you no longer have access to your original number, contact customer support for identity verification and manual update.

Question: What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access to my account?
Answer: If you notice unfamiliar bookings, login activity, or profile changes you did not make, change your account password or request a new OTP-based login immediately, and contact customer support to report the issue and secure your account.
