

CONTACT INFORMATION

This document outlines the general contact channels available for customers to reach Elixway for support, feedback, or business inquiries. Specific contact numbers, email addresses, and live chat availability are maintained within the app and website, as these details may be updated periodically and are not fixed within this knowledge document.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT CHANNELS

Elixway provides multiple channels through which customers can reach support:

- In-app support, accessible directly through the Help or Support section of the Elixway app, offering both automated assistance and access to human support agents for more complex issues.
- Customer support phone line, provided in booking confirmations and within the app, for urgent or trip-related concerns requiring immediate attention.
- Email support, suitable for non-urgent inquiries, documentation requests, or detailed issue descriptions that may require supporting attachments.
- Live chat, where available, offering real-time assistance for booking questions, policy clarifications, and general support needs.

WHEN TO CONTACT SUPPORT

Customers are encouraged to contact support for matters such as:

- Questions about an existing or upcoming booking.
- Issues encountered during a trip, including driver communication concerns or route issues.
- Payment or billing discrepancies.
- Requests related to cancellations, refunds, or policy exceptions.
- General questions about services, vehicle categories, or coverage areas.

URGENT VERSUS NON-URGENT INQUIRIES

For urgent matters, particularly those involving an ongoing or imminent trip, customers should use the in-app support option or the customer support phone line for the fastest response. Non-urgent inquiries, such as general feedback or documentation requests, are well suited to email support.

BUSINESS AND CORPORATE INQUIRIES

Customers or organizations interested in corporate account setups, bulk booking arrangements, or partnership inquiries should reach out through the dedicated business inquiry channel available on the Elixway website, where such requests are directed to the appropriate team for follow-up.

RESPONSE EXPECTATIONS

Elixway aims to respond to customer support inquiries within a reasonable timeframe appropriate to the urgency of the request, with in-app and phone-based channels generally offering faster response times than email for time-sensitive matters.

FEEDBACK AND SUGGESTIONS

Customers wishing to share feedback about their experience, suggest improvements, or report non-urgent issues with the platform can do so through the feedback option available within the app, which is reviewed by the relevant internal teams.

FINDING UP-TO-DATE CONTACT DETAILS

The most current contact numbers, email addresses, and support hours are always available within the Help or Support section of the Elixway app and website, ensuring customers have access to accurate and current contact information.
